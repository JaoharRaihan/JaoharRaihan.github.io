INDEPENDENT PRODUCT BUILD STAGE 01 / 04

CutBook

Salon & Parlour Management POS System

01

Fast Service Logging & Bilingual UI

Clean React Native interface with large touch targets and full Bengali + English support, designed so stylists and shop owners can log services in two quick taps between clients.

02

Automated Commission & Payout Logic

Client-side calculation engine that automatically splits daily revenue, calculates individual staff percentage shares, and logs shop expenses to eliminate late-night manual bookkeeping.

03

Local Persistence & Offline Workflows

Built around local storage (AsyncStorage, with SQLite under evaluation) so the register remains functional during network dropouts, keeping business records safe directly on the device.

04

Direct Salon Conversations & Pilot Testing

Shaped by in-person discussions with salon owners in Dhaka to learn how they manage cash, track staff, and adopt software. Currently refining workflows and testing features toward a Google Play release.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

“Understanding how software works — and how an idea becomes something people genuinely rely on.”

I'm Jaohar Raihan, a React Native developer and product builder from Dhaka, Bangladesh. I graduated with a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB) in 2024.

I started programming because I wanted to understand how software works under the hood and how concepts turn into interactive applications. Over time, that curiosity shifted from writing isolated code toward building complete products that solve real problems.

In my professional roles at OrangeToolz and Akij iBOS, I worked on mobile applications like Agent GoGo, SalesPype CRM, and Akij Dealer Khata. Those experiences taught me how real engineering teams maintain existing codebases, integrate APIs, structure application state, and troubleshoot edge cases.

Alongside refining my fundamentals, I'm building CutBook — a salon and parlour management POS created after talking directly with local shop owners who still rely on paper notebooks. I'm learning by building, testing in the real world, and improving with every step.

PERSPECTIVE

More than writing code

Building CutBook and working on business applications changed how I think about software engineering.

A feature isn't finished when the screen renders. It has to make someone's daily work easier, fit naturally into their habits, handle spotty connections, and be understandable to someone who doesn't work in tech.

I care about the problem, the user, the business model, and what happens after release — not just the code block.

APPROACH

Still building. Still learning.

I am early in my career and don't pretend to have every answer or know every tool at a senior level.

What I do bring is curiosity, ownership, and consistency. I learn by building real projects, speaking with real users, making mistakes, taking feedback, and showing up to build better the next day.

I want to become an engineer who can take an idea from a rough sketch to a dependable product that people genuinely use.
