FLAGSHIP PRODUCT · ACTIVE IN PRODUCTION
MOBILE PRODUCT & POS SYSTEM

CutBook — Professional Salon Management & POS

✂️

CutBook is an active mobile product engineered to eliminate the daily operational chaos of beauty parlours, barbershops, and salons. Many small salon businesses in Bangladesh still manage appointments, staff commission shares, inventory, and cash settlements manually across physical ledgers or fragmented notes. CutBook brings all operations into one intuitive, offline-first mobile app.

Service Management: Real-time customer turnaround logs
Staff & Payouts: Automated commission tiers & salaries
Expenses: Daily overhead & inventory tracking
Multiple Salons: Multi-branch management in one login
Bilingual UI: Bengali & English tailored for local users
React Native TypeScript Node.js MongoDB Mobile POS Offline-First

HOLISTIC PERSPECTIVE

Beyond the Code

Writing performant code is table stakes. Creating a lasting product requires curiosity across every touchpoint of human and business behavior.

01

Product Design

How should the experience feel?

Eliminating friction before it starts. Micro-interactions, haptic responses, and clean layout hierarchies that make complex tasks feel second nature.

02

Users

What problem are we actually solving?

Stepping away from assumptions. Spending time observing real people in their work environment to see where their pain points truly lie.

03

Business

Can the product become sustainable?

Balancing customer value with economic reality. Pricing models, operational margins, and unit economics that ensure longevity.

04

Distribution

How will people discover it?

A great app with zero users is just code. Finding targeted channels, word-of-mouth incentives, and direct onboarding methods.

05

Iteration

What can be improved after real feedback?

Rejecting ego. Embracing analytics, crash logs, and honest user critique to rapidly refine each subsequent release.
