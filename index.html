FLAGSHIP PRODUCT BUILT FOR REAL EVERYDAY USE ACTIVE IN DEVELOPMENT

CutBook

Professional Salon Management & POS System

CutBook is a mobile product I'm building for salon and parlour owners to manage their everyday business more simply.

Core Operations in One Place

Work entries, staff earnings, commissions, payouts, expenses, payments, and business history — brought together in one place.

বাংলা + English Built with Bengali and English users in mind, with support for managing multiple salons.

Why I built it

I wanted to build something for a type of business I see around me every day.

Many small salons still manage important parts of their daily work manually. CutBook started from that simple observation.

Building it has taken me beyond writing code — I've had to think about the product, pricing, payments, user feedback, distribution, and how a real business would actually use it.

This is exactly the kind of product-building experience I want to keep exploring.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

“Understanding how an idea becomes a product people use in their everyday life.”

I'm Jaohar, a React Native developer from Dhaka, Bangladesh.

I started with code because I wanted to understand how software works. Over time, I became more interested in what happens after the code is written — how an idea becomes a product, how people use it, and how it can solve something in their everyday life.

That's what I'm exploring now: building useful software and learning from the process.

PERSPECTIVE

More than writing code

Building my own products has changed the way I think about development.

I now think about the problem, the user, the experience, the business model, the distribution, and the feedback — not just the implementation.
